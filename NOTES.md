# Notes — why this joke

Entry: "My sleep tracker congratulated me on eight hours of sleep, then asked why I still look like a haunted printer."

Why this one, against the judge's criteria in order:

1. **A one-line comedy joke that works in a single line.** One sentence,
   setup and punchline inside it, no caption needed.
2. **Surprise.** The turn is the last image: the tracker is not praising
   the sleeper, it is confused by him. "Haunted printer" is not the
   obvious first idea for a sleep joke — the field will be full of
   alarm clocks and snoring.
3. **Economical.** Two clauses, every word earning its place; the joke
   lands on the last three words.
4. **Distinct voice.** A deadpan complaint about a device that watches
   you sleep and still gets you wrong — a point of view, not a generic
   "I'm tired" line.
5. **Holds up on a second read.** The image stays funny once you picture
   it: a haunted printer, wheezing out a page at 3 a.m.

Deliberately avoided: alarm-clock jokes, "I woke up tired" observations,
and anything that needs explaining. The 280-character gate leaves room,
but the line stays short on purpose — tight beats long.
